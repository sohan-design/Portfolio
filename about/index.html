About Me

Some quick facts about me

Hi, I’m Sohan, a product designer who lives in enterprise SaaS, dev, and AI tools. I’ve been designing for four years, going on five, and I still get a kick out of it. Impact, creativity, and the particular pain of bad UX are why I became a designer in the first place.

None of this was a straight line. It started in a place where nobody was talking about interfaces, least of all me.

A few detours before design…

  1. 1

    When I was young I was deep into calligraphy. That habit turned into graphic design. I loved how white type could sit on a good landscape and make the whole thing feel finished. I was also quietly obsessed with operating systems and their interfaces. I just didn’t know yet that interfaces were a thing you could spend a life on.

  2. 2

    I grew up in Allapalli, a remote pocket on the Maharashtra–Telangana border where the cultures blend into each other. So remote that BSNL was the only network in town. The place is a kind of bliss, except the signal. I only understood the bliss later, once city life arrived and drowned it out.

  3. 3

    A boy from a village, sitting in an internet cafe, finds out there is a degree called Architecture. I think I was the first person in a 200km radius who actually chased design as a career. I was in. Then, somewhere in the third semester, I realized designing spaces just didn’t float my boat.

  4. 4

    So I quit. I picked a plain computer science degree, and I loved the logic of how code worked. Finally there was room to poke at the place where design meets the machine. I taught myself the rest, and eventually I landed a product design job.

Where I am today…

That job turned into a career I’m proud of. Four years on, I’ve gone from teaching myself the basics to shaping real products. The way I work now looks nothing like the kid lettering in a notebook.

With AI rewriting the job, I believe in myself as a designer more than ever. I remember how boxed-in it used to feel, capped by whatever we could push around on a canvas. Now, if we can imagine it, we can actually build it. That makes us the most powerful generation of designers to ever exist, and I can’t wait to see what we do with it.
