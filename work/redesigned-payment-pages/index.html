Edmingle.com Redesign

A site that finally leads with AI

I rebuilt edmingle.com so the website told the same story as the product — enterprise-ready, with AI on the front page.

The Problem

Edmingle had shipped a wave of new features, especially AI, but the website still spoke to individual creators. Enterprise buyers were arriving to a colourful, busy site that buried the product’s real story.

Creator-era website

Busy, colourful, and aimed at solo educators — not institutions.

AI buried

The headline capability lived in a features dropdown instead of the lead story.

The product had moved forward. The website needed to catch up — and lead with AI.

Good

  • Strong new AI product capabilities
  • Founders aligned on the mandate
  • Clear one-month window to ship

Bad

  • Site still creator-focused
  • AI not on the homepage
  • Inbound still asking “can I use this for my course?”

01

We shipped a new hero as an MVP in week one, then rolled the system across Features, Pricing, and AI.

Cleaner type, org messaging, and AI as the lead story validated the direction fast. Once the hero landed, the same language and layout spread through the core conversion pages instead of waiting for a perfect full redesign.

Visit live website

02

We built paid-ad landing pages and ran Figma, Framer, and code in parallel so the month didn’t become a waterfall.

Ad pages kept a single CTA and minimal nav. Astra on WordPress held Core Web Vitals for ad quality. Design and development moved together — hi-fi in Figma, prototypes in Framer, then production — while the Four Pillars IA waited for phase two.

The Result

Within weeks the audience shifted. Inbound moved from “can I use this for my course?” to “how does your AI work for our institution?” The website finally told the same story the product did — and enterprise buyers noticed.
