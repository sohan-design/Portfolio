About Cosmofeed

Cosmofeed is a Forbes recognised startup that allows creators to build, manage, and monetize their online business. It has processed earnings of Rs 100 crore for over 5,000 creators in FY23.

Introduction to Payment Pages

Cosmofeed Payment Pages is a tool designed to help creators & small businesses create and customize payment pages and sell digital products.

It was designed and implemented within the span of 1 week to learn from the market and iterate. In just 6 months, the product became the highest-revenue-driving tool for Cosmofeed, despite lacking a good experience!

Redesign was required because…

  1. We were receiving majority of the support tickets for this product.
  2. The product became the priority for business growth and there were a lot of gaps in its experience and flow!

Product Goals to achieve through redesign…

  1. Improve navigation and layout
  2. Rethink certain use cases and features and optimize the experience for our target audience
  3. Make the design scalable for the future
  4. Redesign the UI elements to fit the new branding
Main goal of the users: To sell products and make money online by creating highly convertible payment pages.

Research

Due to a time crunch, I relied mainly on internal research: I spoke to Business Development and Customer Support team, checked out existing Payment Pages and did Competitive Analysis.

What did we find after all?

  1. Several features were not being used at all or were being misunderstood — like goal tracker, activate sales, etc.
  2. There was a steep rise in the number of frauds due to unfulfilled orders.
  3. Payment Page became an easy tool to sell illegal stuff.
  4. Catalog — a feature that allowed users to list multiple products on a single page (like an e-store), was not being used as much as we expected.
  5. There was a lack of customization in the look & feel of the page and the content it presents.

Addressing the concerns

1. Optimizing as per User Goals

Payment Pages was trying to cover several use cases without optimizing it for the direct target audience.

For the redesigned version, we went back to the basics and adjusted our product strategy to focus on our direct users first.

Currently, it includes only Exit Intent popup feature, but in the next few months, we will be adding new features such as bump offer, cross-sell/upsell and countdown.

2. Addressing Unethical Activities

Frauds:

That’s why we decided to discontinue physical products as a type until we integrate a shipping service solution.

Product Policies:

Entering product policies was an important step while creating a Payment Page. It helped in maintaining transparency between the creators and the buyers and thus, reduced the frauds.

We increased the visibility of entering product policies so that buyers can learn about refunds and fulfilment directly from the creators.

Sale of illegal items:

Another major concern was the sale of illegal stuff. To deal with this, we made our Terms and Conditions clearer and gave creators easier access to them.

We also talked to the support team to strictly review the pages and suspend them if needed.

3. Promoting Customisation & Flexibility

Earlier, the only customisation that Payment Pages allowed was change in color and dark theme.

With the revamped version, we aim to make Payment Pages more flexible in terms of color, aesthetics, content and layout.

4. Dealing with Catalog

This feature was introduced in March 2023 when the team felt a demand for the ability to list multiple products on a single payment page. However, it fell short of our expectations.

Due to these reasons, we decided to discontinue the Catalog feature in our revamped creation flow.

We gave it a separate entry point in the pre-creation process and will test how the feature performs for some time. Depending on its performance, we may either remove it entirely from the Payment Page or consider its inclusion in the future.

5. Improving Layout and making Design Scalable for the future

From what I learned through the research, creators found it difficult to navigate the creation flow. One had to explore the entire flow several times to get the hang of where features are located!

With the revamped version, the goal was to simplify the flow & IA and make sure the design is highly-extensive for future updates!

This is what we ended up with!

Now let’s talk numbers!

The redesigned version was launched in August 2023, and we tracked the product performance for 2–3 months.

  1. No. of transactions: In just 2 months, Payment Pages transactions increased from 28,000/month to 150,000/month, which is almost a 4.3x increase.
  2. Amount transacted on Payment Pages: Amount transacted on Payment Pages by the users increased by 8x in 2 months!
  3. Support tickets: Number of support tickets for Payment Pages declined substantially by 70%.

Feedback received from the team

Major Platform Update

Stakeholders and the team loved the layout and UI of the redesigned version so much that we have now decided to make this creation flows’ layout common for all 5 of our products (Events, Courses, 1-on-1, etc.).

I think I can safely say that this project was a big hit for Cosmofeed and I :) Payment Pages has also maintained its position as the highest-revenue driving tool for us! (as of Dec. 2023)

Reflection

Revamping Payment Pages was a very special and important project for me & Cosmofeed. It feels like the onset of a new journey!

I have learned the nuances of the product design industry and how creating a product is such a collaborative process. It can never be done in isolation, and how I needed to communicate and align/realign with all the stakeholders at every step of the way!

I also understood the importance of delivering designs at speed for a startup. In a perfect world, I would have conducted usability testing, or launched for beta testing, or gone out and talked to people first, but oh well, I wish timelines were favourable for the same!
