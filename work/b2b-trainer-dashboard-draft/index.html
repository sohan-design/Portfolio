Rebranding Journey

Building a brand that stays new

I reimagined the company’s new vision to its brand and products.

The Problem

With time, the vision and goals of the company elevated to a wider approach. And to correspond that, a brand and product were absolutely needed for growth and scale. The older brand didn't get the people's trust in first look. As well as, the product didn't feel ready for a launch to the international world.

Old branding

Didn’t earn trust at first glance.

No brand and design systems

Nothing built to scale an international launch.

The product was built for sales. No brand recognition or self onboarding or a scalable system was present to grow further.

Good

  • Extensive utilitarian suite for LMS
  • Customizability
  • White-labelled

Bad

  • Search and discoverability
  • Brand value
  • No design system

01

We rebuilt the whole brand with a new logo, website and the revamped products.

The company had outgrown a sales-led look. I treated the logo, the marketing site, and the product chrome as one job, so a trainer meeting the brand for the first time would recognize the same company inside the dashboard. The new system gave sales a story they could open a call with, and gave every later screen a place to belong.

02

For the international market, the products were revamped, which included the enterprise B2B dashboard for training, a learner dashboard, a learner mobile app which ran on Flutter.

A new logo was not enough for trainers outside the first market. I rebuilt the enterprise admin that trainers and managers live in, then carried that system into the learner dashboard and the Flutter learner app. The B2B dashboard stopped being a legacy tool someone had to be trained on. A new customer could see the operation, onboard themselves, and run training without a specialist on the call.

The Result

The brand and the product landed together. Sales rose after the dashboard modernization, and more trainers started on their own because the product finally explained itself. The same system let us keep shipping instead of redrawing every screen.
