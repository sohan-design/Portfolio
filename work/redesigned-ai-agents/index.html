About UnifyApps

UnifyApps is a fast-growing B2B SaaS startup building a GenAI-first enterprise platform. Its products enable companies to create GenAI apps, manage data, and automate workflows.

Phase I of Advanced AgentsAug 2024

When UnifyApps first launched AI Agents, the goal was simple: help enterprises automate workflows and deliver personalized experiences at scale. It gave technical teams deep control and flexibility, but at a heavy cost:

  1. Setting up a single agent took 25–30 minutes
  2. The flow had too many steps
  3. It relied heavily on technical knowledge
For technical teams, it worked. But for business leaders, the very people who needed to see quick value, it became a barrier. Demo calls were high, but conversions and adoption stayed low.
***********

The Problem

Advanced Agents was powerful but too technical, time-consuming, and taxing. Conversions & adoption stalled because the product didn’t support non-technical teams or guide users through the setup process.

Why this problem mattered for Unifyapps?

  1. Unifyapps recently raised $20M to distribute AI Agents to enterprises.
  2. AI Agents is a core strategic product which directly impacts company’s revenue and market positioning in the AI race.
  3. Fixing usability was critical to client adoption.
***********

Phase II: Dubai SprintJan 2025

By Jan 2025, leadership pressure had reached its peak. The goal was clear: AI Agents had to become radically simpler. Within three days, we were on a flight to Dubai for an intense 3 week sprint with the C-suite.

To address both ends of the user base, we decided a dual model:

  • Simple Agents: newly introduced for non-technical teams, designed for minimal setup to serve enterprise’s small use cases.
  • Advanced Agents: retained for the 20–25% of complex use cases where technical teams needed deep customization and control.

Fluid Interface

Inspired by Medium and Google Docs, we reimagined simple agent creation as a document-like interface — fluid, conversational, and far less intimidating. Instead of filling rigid forms, users could simply ‘write out’ their ideas naturally, almost like drafting an essay.

This shift turned what had been a 30-minute technical process into something fast, intuitive, and empowering!
***********

Solving for 3TsTechnical, Time-consuming & Taxing

1. Technical

Since AI is still a relatively new industry, many users are unfamiliar with its patterns and technical jargons.

For Simple Agents, we focused on simplifying the experience by minimizing jargon and using clear, user-friendly language.

2. Time-Consuming

In Advanced Agents, users had to navigate a lengthy form with too many configuration steps. Many felt it was time-consuming, especially when they just wanted to spin up a quick agent.

For Simple Agents, we streamlined the journey by:

  • Introducing a split-screen view for quicker configuration and testing
  • Adding ‘Generate with AI’ to instantly draft instructions and conversation starters, saving users time
  • Keeping only the essential settings required for creating a simple agent

This reduced friction, saved time, and made the flow feel significantly faster!

3. Taxing & Intimidating

Many users described Advanced Agents as intimidating and overwhelming.

For Simple Agents, our goal was to make the experience feel as natural as writing a document or mapping out the workflow they envisioned for their agent!

Reflection

This project was a turning point for me as a designer. It wasn’t just about simplifying an interface — it was about reframing how people interact with AI.

We didn’t play it safe. Instead of tweaking forms, we experimented with a document-like interface — a format we’d never tried before and weren’t sure could handle complex flows. But stepping outside the familiar was exactly what made the breakthrough possible.

What I learned is that sometimes adoption requires courage to break patterns. By moving beyond convention, we made AI Agents not just powerful for technical teams, but approachable for business leaders too.

And in the end, that’s what mattered most: turning something intimidating into something empowering!
